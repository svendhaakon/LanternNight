Lantern Night

Keep your lantern burning in the dark.

Shop
Cosmetics
How to play
  • Move: use the mouse, drag a finger, or press the arrow keys. Your lantern follows smoothly.
  • Goal: your light shrinks over time. Catch fireflies to refill it and score points.
  • Combo: catch fireflies within 2 seconds of each other to build a combo and get bonus light.
  • Coins: every point you score is worth 1 🪙 coin. Spend coins in the Shop on upgrades and in Cosmetics on looks.
  • Game over: you lose when your light runs out or a bat touches you.
Power-ups
Bats & tips
  • Bats are hidden in the dark. You only see them inside your light, so a bigger light gives earlier warning.
  • More bats arrive, and they get faster, as the night goes on.
  • Bat-killing power-ups: Burn, Shield, Nova, Zap, Meteors, and Blades. Each bat you destroy gives +3 coins.
  • Mutant bats are green and bigger, and you can spot their glowing eyes even in the dark. They are tougher: Burn takes about 2 seconds, Zap needs two hits, Blades need a few touches, a Shield only knocks them back, and Nova or a Meteor direct hit does the most. Each one is worth +10 coins.
  • Orbs disappear after about 14 seconds, and they blink just before they go.
  • Tip: save Nova for when bats surround you, and grab Nectar when your light is low.
